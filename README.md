## Investment Thesis

Apple combines a substantial hardware business with a growing services segment. In FY2025, iPhone remained its largest product line at approximately $209.6 billion of revenue, while Services generated approximately $109.2 billion, or 26.2% of total revenue. Services growth and Apple’s integrated ecosystem support the business, but hardware replacement cycles, competitive pressure, and valuation sensitivity remain important.

The exit-multiple DCF suggests modest downside against the model’s reference price, while the perpetuity-growth approach suggests substantially greater downside. This wide range reflects the sensitivity of intrinsic value to terminal assumptions. **Conclusion: a conditional HOLD**, pending refreshed market inputs and a reassessment of terminal valuation assumptions.

## Key Research Highlights

- **Business mix:** FY2025 Services revenue grew 13.5%; iPhone contributed approximately 50.4% of total revenue.
- **Financial performance:** FY2025 revenue was approximately $416.2 billion, with a five-year revenue CAGR of 8.7%.
- **Profitability:** FY2025 gross margin was approximately 46.9%, EBIT margin 32.0%, and net margin 26.9%.
- **Industry analysis:** Smartphones, laptops, tablets, wearables, accessories, and digital services.
- **Valuation:** Five-year forecast of unlevered free cash flow, an EBITDA exit-multiple DCF, a perpetuity-growth DCF, and trading-comparables research.
- **Risks:** Hardware concentration, terminal-multiple sensitivity, services growth uncertainty, and advanced semiconductor manufacturing constraints.
